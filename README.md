### 🌱 Welcome to my GitHub!

My focus areas include <strong>carbon audit and flux modeling, green hydrogen / CCUS, energy systems analysis,

I lead <strong><a href="https://github.com/liulinda185-spec/carbon-falcon-ocip">Carbon Falcon / OCIP</a></strong>,

## 🚀 Selected Projects

- **[carbon-falcon-ocip](https://github.com/liulinda185-spec/carbon-falcon-ocip)** — Open-source carbon flux audit platform for GBA enterprises: a 7-step falsification audit pipeline with conservation reconciliation, leakage detection and uncertainty analysis. [Live demo](https://carbon-falcon-8dubwckd7mfseynb7azamc.streamlit.app/) · 118 tests · Apache-2.0 · companion to the SSRN preprint.
- **[cityu-facility-report](https://github.com/liulinda185-spec/cityu-facility-report)** — AI-assisted campus facility reporting and triage MVP: mobile ticket reporting with photo upload, TF-IDF + logistic regression routing (93% held-out accuracy), officer console and operations dashboard with a closed feedback loop. [Live demo](https://cityu-facility-report.onrender.com)
